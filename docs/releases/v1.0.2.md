GyroAim maps the DualSense's gyro onto the sticks, so you can aim or steer by
moving the controller in games that were never built to know it can.

## Added

- **Hold a rotation instead of flowing it.** Tilt the pad and the stick stays
  over until you bring it back, which is what steering wants rather than
  aiming.
- **Any rotation to any control.** Turning, tilting and leaning each route to
  either stick, either axis, either direction, and to more than one at once.
- **A button condition can say which button.** Pick from a chip per button, or
  hold the ones you want and have them taken from your hands.
- **Reset to defaults**, back to plain aiming on the right stick with the left
  trigger.
- **The panel keeps the service current.** Updating the app no longer leaves an
  older service running behind it.

## Fixed

- **Settings leaked from one game into the next**, and a game with no profile
  of its own saved over the general settings. Profiles saved by earlier
  versions may already hold values that leaked this way.
- **A held angle drifted over a session** until it sat past full scale and
  stopped responding one way. Tilting and leaning are now measured against
  gravity, which cannot drift.
- **The resting gyro offset was never learned** with the condition set to
  Always, leaving a creep in every reading.
- **Large games could not be attached to**, RPCS3 among them.
- **The settings page went dead while aiming kept working**, on nothing more
  than one silent browser connection.
- **A game left half-hooked could not be hooked again.**
- **Two copies of the service could run at once.**
- **The routing grid rendered empty.**
